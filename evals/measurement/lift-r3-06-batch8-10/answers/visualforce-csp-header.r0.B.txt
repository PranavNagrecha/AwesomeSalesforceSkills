**What to do in practice**
1. For each page, check whether `<apex:page>` sets `cspHeader="true"`. If it doesn't, the org's trusted-site list isn't restricting that page, and external `<apex:includeScript>` or `<apex:stylesheet>` sources load unrestricted.
2. Where you turn it on, first inventory every external host the page loads from. Then add `CspTrustedSite` entries (context `VisualForce`), or move the assets into static resources, so you don't break the page.
3. Test as the lowest-privilege audience (community or guest) and watch the browser console for CSP violations.
4. Treat enabling CSP as hardening for the *harden-in-place* set, especially pages exposed to external users. It complements FLS and CSRF fixes rather than replacing them.
