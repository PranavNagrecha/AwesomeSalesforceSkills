**10,000 notification actions per hour per org.** The Salesforce developer guide states that a subscriber org can execute up to 10,000 notification actions per hour (pkg2_dev 264 L5916). The older figure of 1,000 that circulates is wrong.

A few points that matter in practice:

- **Unit of count:** the limit is on notification *actions*, not recipients. Whether one action fanned out to many users counts as one is not confirmed in the corpus, so verify it before designing around it.
- **Overflow behavior:** the commonly quoted Help text says that once the cap is hit, no more notifications are sent that hour, unsent ones are lost, and sending resumes the next hour. That is a silent drop, not an exception. I couldn't verify this against the corpus, so treat it as Help-only. Don't rely on a fault connector to detect it. Build your own instrumentation instead, such as a counter object (the `Notification_Log__c` approach in Pattern 3) or a digest design.
- **Bulk flows:** a 200-record load with several cascading automations that each send a notification can exhaust the budget quickly. Gate bulk sends or aggregate to a digest.
- **Related caps, not to be confused:** title is 250 characters and body 750 (Apex `CustomNotification` limits). Clients may truncate the displayed text earlier. Email has a separate limit of 5,000 external addresses per day.
