## Deploy order — one row that could not be placed

The ten-position milestone sequence is objects, fields, picklists, record types, layouts, permission
sets, sharing, automation, routing, SLA. `CWB-PERM-002` is **6 of 10** (permission sets), position 2
inside the step. `CWB-PERM-001` has no slot at all: the sequence has no position for a
`CustomPermission`. Step 5 says a row that cannot be placed is reported rather than quietly given a
number, so it is reported — and the constraint that actually governs it is local and absolute rather
than sequential: the definition ships before the grant, because a `PermissionSet` naming a custom
permission that does not yet exist fails the deploy.

The step's own cross-milestone ordering runs the other way and is the more dangerous one:
**this step must land before `M3-S01`**. A validation rule whose `$Permission.X` names a permission
that does not exist does not fail the deploy — it evaluates to `false` silently — so every
API-created Case would be blocked from the moment `M3-S01` landed until this step did.
