## 6. The blocking finding — F-M4S01-01

```text
ISSUE: BusinessHours.settings-meta.xml / calendar 'Severity 1 24x7': every day is 00:00:00.000Z to
00:00:00.000Z — this is the shipped 24/7 shape; SLA clocks on this calendar never pause.
```

The rule (`check_business_hours_and_holidays.py`, `windows == 7 and all_midnight`) exists to catch an
org that never edited the shipped 24/7 `Default` calendar. Here the same shape is the deliberate
expression of `requirement.md` L18 and of `steps[M4-S01].inputs.calendars["Severity 1 24x7"]`, and it is
the only always-open form the cited skill documents. Two variants were run to bound the finding:

| Variant (scratch copies, not artefacts) | Exit | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `Severity 1 24x7` removed | 0 | every other rule passes — the 14 holidays, their attachments, the single active default, the absent weekends, the `startTime`/`endTime` pairing |
| `Severity 1 24x7` trimmed to Mon–Fri | 0 | the gate can be passed by closing a never-pausing calendar at weekends; recorded so a later run does not adopt it as a fix |

A human decides, and either is a one-line change: amend the step to drop the third calendar and let
M4-S04's `businessHoursSource = None` carry the 24/7 promise (its own `inputs.note` already specifies
that entry), or raise the checker rule to INFO for a non-default always-open calendar — a skill change,
the same shape as commit `214da9abd`. `artefacts/M4-S01/deploy-order.md` § 0 carries the full
argument, including the third rejected repair and its cost.
