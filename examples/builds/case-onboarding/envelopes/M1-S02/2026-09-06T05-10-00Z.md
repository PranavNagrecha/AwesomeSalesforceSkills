**A parser fault found while writing the Section 2 rows, and why one file was fixed and another was
not.** `check_workbook.py` splits a table row on every bare `|` (script L330) with no escape
handling, so an RTM-style `\|` inside a cell shifts every column after it — the linter then reads
`source_req_id` as `recommended_agent` and reports four errors that are all one formatting fault.
The Section 2 rows were written with `;`, the delimiter `recommended_skills` already documents, and
they now produce zero per-row findings. M1-S01's three rows in `workbook/99-other-configuration.md`
use `\|` and are **not** caught, because the **Other configuration** heading is not a canonical
section and the parser skips the file's rows entirely — those rows have never been row-checked by
anything. They were left byte-identical (Step 8: rows belonging to another step are not this run's
to rewrite) and the latent break is flagged in that file and here for `M5-S04`, which is the run that
compiles them into a document a parser will read.
