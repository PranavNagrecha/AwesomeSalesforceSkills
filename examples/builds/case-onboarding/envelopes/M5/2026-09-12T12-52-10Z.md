### The deploy-order divergence, resolved

`artefacts/M5-S04/deploy-order.md` puts `Settings:Case` **before** the assignment and auto-response
rules; `artefacts/M5-S05/deploy-order.md` puts it **after** (rules slot 10, `Settings:Case` slot 11).

**`M5-S05` is right**, on four grounds: `artefacts/M3-S03/deploy-order.md` § 3 states the
rules-first sequence without qualification; `CWB-AUT-007`'s own note gives as its reason an
argument *against* its own stated position; the compiled table is plan-step order (a record of
assembly) rather than dependency order (a deploy instruction); and the downside is asymmetric and
one-way — Email-to-Case, once enabled, cannot be disabled.

Three qualifications so it is not over-read: neither order is a deploy error (one `deploy()` call,
platform resolves dependencies within the request); the real safety margin is the **human** step
after slot 11 (mail forwarding, form publication); and on a greenfield org with no forwarding,
neither order can produce unrouted traffic. It matters for a **split release**, or where forwarding
is already live.

The doc keeper was right to *report* the conflict rather than resolve it (its Step 10 says so).
The resolution should not now live only in this report — a line belongs in `M5-S04`'s hazard list
at the next touch.
