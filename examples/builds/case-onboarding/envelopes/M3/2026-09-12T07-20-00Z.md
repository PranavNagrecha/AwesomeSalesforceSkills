## Retracted recommendation

**What was said.** The first report offered, under G3 decision item 1, a 'third option neither decision names': replyToEmail = support@acme.example with senderEmail left at support-noreply@, on the reasoning that the loop is a From-address mechanism.

**Why it is wrong.** skills/admin/assignment-rules/references/gotchas.md Gotcha 6 states the loop pattern as an autoResponseRules entry's 'senderEmail (or replyToEmail)' matching a routing address — an out-of-office or bounce auto-reply is addressed to Reply-To, so the mail returns to the routing address by exactly that route. check_assignment_rules.py rules AR-LOOP-01 and AR-SENDER-01 treat the two elements as one class.

**Availability.** The clause was committed in 0c6a13440 at 2026-09-11 23:48, about six hours before the first run. It was readable at the time and was missed.

**Second error.** 'Neither decision names it' was also wrong: decisions.md D-M3S04-04 names this exact alternative and rejects it on the same grounds.

**Consequence for the build.** None. artefacts/M3-S04/autoResponseRules/Case.autoResponseRules-meta.xml is correct exactly as built; the reply-threading cost in D-M3S04-04 is a consequence to accept, not a defect to engineer around.
