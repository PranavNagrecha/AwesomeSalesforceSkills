`Application_Log__c` and `Logger_Setting__mdt` that no step ships — was decided at the plan,
**option (b)**. Three requester decisions are now bound in `inputs{}`:

| Decision | Code change |
|---|---|
| `include_logger: false`; the visible log **is** `Integration_Failure__c` (Q16–Q18), everything else `System.debug` | `ApplicationLogger.cls` + `-meta.xml` **deleted** from the package; every call is now `System.debug(LoggingLevel.…)` — `ERROR` on the three failure paths, `INFO`/`WARN` on the run outcome |

| `WITH USER_MODE` on an aggregate stays **UNVERIFIED** | clause still omitted; the `// reason:` comment now names the UNVERIFIED status, and `deploy-order.md` § 4 item 2 records it |

A round-1 ambiguity closed itself: with no logging requirement left, `execute()` is a single
`System.enqueueJob` statement and nothing else — the literal reading of D11.
