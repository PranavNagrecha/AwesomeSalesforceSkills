## 2. The lesson, recorded because it is the valuable half

Run 2 shipped `URL.getSalesforceBaseUrl()` and the name passed Gate C check 2 **legitimately**:
three skills in this repo spell it, so the grep that backs identifier provenance returned a
hit. What none of those three carries is the **version gate**. The method was removed after
58.0; these classes are pinned to 67.0.

A repo grep answers *"does the corpus spell this name?"*. The question that decides whether
a package deploys is *"is this name legal at the `apiVersion` this step stamps into its own
meta XML?"* — and the second is the one `AGENT_CONTRACT.md` rule 12 is really about.
**Attestation is necessary and it is not sufficient.**

Nothing offline caught it. Not the three checkers, not the XML parse, not the brace scan,
not the identifier grep. **Only the org did.** The three skills that carry the removed
method are a library defect the operator has logged as S2-F-04; they are not this step's to
edit.
