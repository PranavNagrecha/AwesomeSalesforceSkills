## 6. Where AGENT.md and the CLI did not support § 3.1 at `feature`

1. **The five-step ceiling has no carve-out for the step § 4 prescribes.** § 3.1 gives the
   `feature` tier one milestone and ≤ 5 steps, with an explicit carve-out for the
   aggregating Apex manifest step. § 4's Apex row note says a plan with more than one Apex
   step sharing a template dependency runs a dedicated **Apex foundations** step first.
   This plan has two Apex steps and needed one — so a correctly-shaped `feature` plan here
   needs six functional steps plus the manifest step, and § 3.1 carves out only the latter.
   `validate` reports the overrun as a WARN, so the plan reads green either way.
2. **`templates[]` is validated for existence, never for what the template needs.** Nothing
   in `build_plan.py`, the plan verifier's three lenses, or this runner's Step 5 opens a
   cited template to check its own references. Both Apex steps of this build were blocked
   by that gap. A cheap fix exists: a plan-time check that greps each cited
   `templates/apex/**` file for other template class names and for `__c` / `__mdt` tokens,
   and WARNs when the closure is not covered by some step's `outputs[]`.
3. **The runner's persistence contract and the build layout disagree.** Wave 10 names
   `docs/reports/build-step-runner/<run_id>.{md,json}` alongside the build-scoped envelope;
   § 2 puts agent envelopes under `envelopes/<step-id>/`. The invocation constrained writes
   to the build directory, so the build-scoped pair was written and the `docs/reports/`
   pair was not. Recorded rather than resolved.
