three named files; `DateTime` and `Checkbox` resolve in none of them, nor in any of

cannot be retrieved or deployed (api_meta.txt:95020–95021), so M1-S02's "every custom field readable
and editable" is eleven fields, not twelve. Recorded in `deploy-order.md` §6; not this step's to fix.

not whether the three are `true` or absent — both clear every checker).

**Healthy.** Every checker passed first time, and the reason is traceable: the verifier's W2 round
put each checker's full exit-1 set into the step inputs before the build ran.
