The testability table's **Human gate present** row fires on a literal reading, and
`REFUSAL_SECURITY_GUARD` says such a plan is *rejected, never verified with a warning*. Three
checked reasons put it on the warning side instead:

1. **§ 3.1 forces the shape.** *"`build-planner` — Step 6 (`ask`: one step, `human_gate: false`)"*,
   and *"`ensure-gates` reads `scale`: at `ask` it adds `milestone:M1` and **no** `step:` record,
   because the single step is written `human_gate: false`."* Refuting here would demand the planner
   violate the contract the plan was built to.
2. **§ 3.1's CRITICAL list at `ask` is exhaustive** and names only executability/grounding
   refutations. A testability finding cannot earn a re-plan at this tier by construction.
3. **`REFUSAL_CODES.md` scopes the guard** to *"a security-sensitive permission (e.g. `Modify All
   Data` on a persona PSG)"*. A named bypass custom permission for two users is the artefact
   `admin/validation-rules` ranks *"Preferred — granular, auditable"*.

The mitigation was **verified, not assumed**: `RUN.md` names both
`customPermissions/Bypass_Opp_Amount_Lock.customPermission-meta.xml` and
`permissionsets/Opp_Amount_Lock_Bypass.permissionset-meta.xml` in `M1-S01`'s outputs and names both
in the manual acceptance line, so the grant is in front of the human at `go`.
