routes to `metadata-builder`; `recommended_agent` below names the step's owning agent
(`metadata-builder`), not the Section 1 default (`object-designer`), per
`agents/build-doc-keeper/AGENT.md` Step 4.

artefact this step produced, sourced from `requirement.md` and the answered clarifications named in
each row's notes; the mapping is recorded once here and reused by every later step and by
`traceability.md`.
