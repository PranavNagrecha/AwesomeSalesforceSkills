## 1. Summary

Repaired M1-S02 after `reports/MOCK-DEPLOY-M1.md` run 1 returned **N3-F-02 (HIGH)** on both layouts:
*"Layout must contain an item for required layout field: Probability."* Added one `layoutItems` entry
to the `Opportunity Information` section of both `artefacts/M1-S02/layouts/*.layout-meta.xml`,
immediately after `Amount`: field `Probability`, `<behavior>Required</behavior>`. Nothing else in
either file changed; `package.xml` is unaffected (no new component or member). `deploy-order.md`
gained § 6 "Repair after run 1". Outcome: **built**.

**Why `Required` and not `Edit`.** The deploy message alone only requires *an item to exist* (the
skill's table maps "no `layoutItems` entry at all" to "add one, any behavior"). But
`admin/record-types-and-page-layouts/references/gotchas.md` #13 establishes that a field the
*platform* requires on the layout is not the analyst's `Edit`/`Required` choice — it is a deploy
precondition, the same shape as `Status` on Case. Writing `Required` now, on the strength of the
org's own finding that `Probability` is platform-required for Opportunity, avoids a foreseeable
second dry-run round that would otherwise report `Field:Probability must be Required`.
