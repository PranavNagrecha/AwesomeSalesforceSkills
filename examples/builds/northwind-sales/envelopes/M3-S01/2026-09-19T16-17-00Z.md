  documents a merge field for approval *comments*, neither sits in a fenced email-template example, and
  neither is in a cited skill — but the premise as written will not survive the grep a reviewer runs.
- *(low, checker-coverage)* `validate` raises the "skill ships a checker nothing runs" WARN for eleven
  other steps but not for M3-S01, and the reason is worth recording rather than reading as a clean bill:
  the suppression is because the M3 *milestone* carries `check_approval_design.py`, which means the
  approval-process half of this step's citation is verified only once M3-S02 exists, and never at step
  scope.

- *(low, action-ordering)* Both outcome bodies merge `{!Opportunity.Approval_Status__c}`, and Q33 puts the
  field update and the email in the same approval event — a `FieldUpdate` and an `Alert` inside one

  The line is worded as an observation of the record (*"Approval status now on the record:"*) rather than

- *(low, cross-step-dependency)* `Discount_Rejected.email` delivers Q33's "with the comments" clause by
