## Q54–Q57 — the per-question call

All four became `assumptions[]` rows; none became a blocking clarification. Two reasons, stated so the human can
overrule at the gate:

1. **Mechanism.** The planner is not a writer of `clarifications[]` — `set-clarifications` is. Inside a plan, "make it
   blocking" is only expressible as a `blocked` step with `blocked_reason: "deferred: Qn"`, and blocking a step is
   warranted only when the step cannot be built without the answer. All four steps can be built.
2. **Grounding.** Each default traces to a sentence already in `requirement.md` — Q55, Q56 and Q57 to item 5 verbatim,
   Q54 to item 6 ("nothing breaks SMB"), whose only non-reaching branch is the one the plan took. And three of the four
   close before deploy inside `M4-S01`'s retrieve runbook.

`A42` (Q56, historical trending) is the one where deferring costs something a later change cannot recover — a reporting
snapshot collects forward only — so its `text` says that in as many words and the capability is now in scope-out rather
than absent.
