**The `$Permission` failure mode is silent in both directions.** `custom-permissions/references/gotchas.md`
#4: a `$Permission.X` naming a permission that is not present evaluates to `false` with "no error, just
silent grant of access to nobody". Inverted by the outer `NOT()`, that means the bypass clause is
permanently `true` and **nobody can bypass the rule, including the sales-ops admin**. The deploy is
green either way. M2-S03 is the first artefact in this build that consumes the permission, which is why
`Consumers 1` above matters — and why even that is only a file-to-file match: the checkers compare
name stems, they cannot prove anyone holds the permission. `PermissionSetAssignment` ships in no
manifest.

`errorDisplayField` carries a fifth, display-only dependency: both M1-S02 layouts carry `Discount__c`
with `behavior Edit`, so the error renders inline. Remove the field from either layout and the message
relocates to Top of Page — no deploy, no warning, no change to this rule.

**Validate-only command for a human** — `metadata-builder` never runs this; it is text to copy. The
