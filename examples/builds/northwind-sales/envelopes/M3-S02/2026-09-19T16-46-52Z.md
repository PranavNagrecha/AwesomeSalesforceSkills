## 6. Deploy-order note — what was added

Full note: `artefacts/M3-S02/deploy-order.md` (now twelve sections, 0–11). New § 9, in brief:

- **Why the email-templates checker test was withdrawn** — `is_template_artefact()` matches
  `*.email`, `*.email-meta.xml`, or a body file under `email/`; this step's four declared
  outputs contain none of those by design, and no checker in this build reads an alert's
  `<template>` from a `.workflow` file, so the withdrawn test asserted nothing about this step
  either before or after removal.
- **Why the entry criteria were narrowed** — a submit-then-recall on an already-Closed-Won or
  Closed-Lost opportunity would fire `Clear_Approval_Status` (`operation` `Null`), and a
  workflow field update re-saves the record without re-running custom validation rules — so the
  recall would commit a closed, above-20%-discount record with a blank Approval Status: the
  state `M2-S03`'s validation rule exists to prevent, reached without the rule running.
  Excluding both closed stages from entry criteria closes the path at the only point this step
  owns. § 6's original "Watch" bullet is left as written, as the record of why the repair was
  made, not as current behaviour.
- **The § 3 `UNVERIFIED (2026-09-19)` marker on `RecordType.DeveloperName` inside a `<formula>`
  entryCriteria stands unchanged** — it was never about `StageName` or clause count, only about
  whether `formula` (versus `criteriaItems`) is legal here at all and whether
  `RecordType.DeveloperName` resolves inside it. Only the M3 mock deploy settles it.
