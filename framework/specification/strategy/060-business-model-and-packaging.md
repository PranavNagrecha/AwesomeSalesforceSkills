# Business model and packaging options

The architecture supports sustainable product options without making the local product intentionally weak.

## Community/local

- specification and schemas;
- existing SfSkills knowledge corpus under its applicable license;
- local runtime and Cursor plugin;
- product-read-only broker;
- fixture/replay mode;
- public benchmark methodology and selected scenarios;
- local run bundles and review tools.

## Team

- shared policy/context/source packs;
- private knowledge overlays;
- approved org/project identities;
- central scenario registry and regression dashboard;
- signed run/review bundles;
- team adapter distribution;
- support for internal evidence sources.

## Enterprise

- private evidence gateway and RBAC;
- audit/retention/data-residency controls;
- managed Salesforce release/source freshness;
- private scenario packs and protected QA orchestration;
- host/model conformance reporting;
- vendor evidence adapters;
- support, SLAs, enablement, and certification.

## Services

- framework adoption and product integration;
- custom evidence adapters;
- customer-specific skill/source governance;
- scenario engineering and scratch-org QA;
- Agentforce quality programs;
- architecture assessments and training.

## Ecosystem

- partner adapters for DevOps, testing, org intelligence, observability, and work management;
- conformance certification for adapters/scenario packs;
- public run/evidence export consumers.

## Principles

- paid packaging extends scale, collaboration, governance, support, and privacy—not factual honesty;
- local users can inspect evidence and methodology;
- no customer must grant write access for diagnostic products;
- specification, adapter, runtime, and corpus licensing are separate decisions;
- business claims require cohort/benchmark evidence.
