# Anti-strategy: what the product must not become

1. **A number-of-skills marketing project.** Inventory does not prove usefulness.
2. **A generic Salesforce code generator.** Official and vendor tooling already competes strongly here.
3. **An unrestricted MCP bundle.** Broad write-capable toolsets increase risk and context noise.
4. **A new full DevOps platform before traction.** Integrate with mature pipelines rather than rebuilding them.
5. **A complete metadata graph claim without a complete graph.** Be explicit about evidence coverage.
6. **Dozens of auto-selectable overlapping subagents.** Use products and focused roles.
7. **A context-window arms race.** Better selection and checkpoints beat indiscriminate loading.
8. **A benchmark tuned to one prompt/model.** Use hidden/rotating truth and cross-host versions.
9. **A report factory without replay.** Every consequential claim needs evidence and run artifacts.
10. **A security story based on model instructions or MCP annotations.** Enforce deny-by-default outside the model.
11. **A customer-org mutation assistant in V2.** Diagnostics and planning remain read-only.
12. **A specification nobody implements.** Every mandatory layer needs a consumer, validator, test, and milestone.
13. **A one-shot Cursor mega-diff.** Local milestone commits, gates, and review packages are mandatory.
14. **A public “40% of market” claim.** Earn measured cohort retention and technical improvement first.
