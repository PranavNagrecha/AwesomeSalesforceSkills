# Design-partner program

## Objective

Prove that SfSkills resolves real Salesforce engineering jobs repeatedly and safely before broad distribution.

## Cohort

Recruit 25–50 practitioners across developers, admins, architects, release engineers, consultants, security reviewers, and Agentforce builders. Include users with and without existing DevOps/org-intelligence platforms. Record host/model and Salesforce environment without collecting credentials or customer payloads.

## Participation path

1. Observe local install and fixture run.
2. Run P01 or P02 on an existing sanitized result.
3. Review evidence and correct wrong/weak findings.
4. Run a second real job within fourteen days.
5. Try P03 or another product relevant to the persona.
6. Export a sanitized replay or defect category.
7. Complete an eight-week retention/trust interview.

## Evidence to collect

- install/doctor outcome and time;
- first-value time;
- normal workflow baseline;
- accepted/rejected claims and recommendations;
- missing evidence/tool/context;
- unsupported claim or reviewer escape;
- context/tool metrics;
- terminal status correctness;
- second run and weekly activity;
- willingness to recommend/pay/deploy to team.

## Guardrails

- no customer credentials in feedback artifacts;
- no raw customer data unless explicitly approved and retained privately;
- no product write access;
- disclose host/model and data handling;
- do not aggregate an accuracy claim across incomparable jobs;
- report cohort denominator and attrition.
