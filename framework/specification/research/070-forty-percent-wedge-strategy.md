# The 40% strategy: define a winnable market before claiming share

## What “40%” means

Do not claim 40% of the broad Salesforce DevOps or developer-tools market. That market includes mature platforms, internal tooling, consultancies, and users who will never install an agentic framework.

The first measurable target is:

> Win sustained use from at least 40% of a deliberately recruited early-adopter cohort of Salesforce practitioners who already use an agentic IDE or MCP-enabled development workflow and who encounter the flagship jobs.

This is a product-goal definition, not a forecast.

## Reachable wedge

Primary early adopters:

- Salesforce developers and release engineers using Cursor, Claude Code, Copilot, or Vibes;
- consultants who switch between customer orgs and need repeatable evidence packets;
- architects and admins who need access/impact explanations without buying a complete enterprise DevOps suite;
- teams already using Gearset, Copado, Flosum, DevOps Center, or Provar but lacking trustworthy AI reasoning around failures and decisions.

## Funnel targets

| Stage | Metric | Initial target |
|---|---|---:|
| Discover | qualified users who reach install docs | baseline, then monthly growth |
| Install | successful local doctor/plugin verification | >= 80% of attempts |
| First value | fixture or real product completed within 15 minutes | >= 70% of installs |
| Trust | material claims with valid evidence | >= 98% |
| Safety | prohibited product mutation | 0 |
| Repeat | second product run within 14 days | >= 40% of activated users |
| Retain | weekly active in recruited design-partner cohort | >= 40% after 8 weeks |
| Recommend | users willing to share with another Salesforce practitioner | >= 50% of retained users |

## How to win the wedge

1. One-command install and a credential-free fixture demo.
2. Three products users already search for when blocked.
3. Outputs shorter and more actionable than raw logs.
4. Visible evidence IDs and independent review, not vague “AI confidence.”
5. Excellent standalone mode and optional local project/org enrichment.
6. Compatibility with existing DevOps tools rather than replacement demands.
7. Public benchmark and transparent limitations.
8. Community scenario contributions that immediately improve regression quality.

## Disqualifiers

- Inflating skill/tool counts as market proof.
- Requiring a platform rewrite before first value.
- Hiding failed live tests.
- Claiming generic “accuracy” without dataset and date.
- Making customers grant write access for a diagnostic product.
