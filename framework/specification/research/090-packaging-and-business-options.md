# Packaging and sustainable product options

## Community/local

- SfSkills corpus and local framework runtime.
- Native Cursor plugin and portable skills.
- Read-only local evidence broker.
- Fixture benchmark and selected public scenarios.
- Self-hosted run bundles.

## Team

- Shared policy packs and org allowlists.
- Central scenario registry and quality dashboard.
- Team review workflow and signed run bundles.
- Private skill/reference overlays.
- CI comparison against approved baselines.

## Enterprise

- Controlled evidence gateway with identity and RBAC.
- Private deployment options and retention controls.
- Audit exports, policy attestation, and support.
- Managed Salesforce release/freshness updates.
- Dedicated known-truth scenario packs.

## Services and ecosystem

- Implementation/enablement.
- Custom product and scenario development.
- Partner adapters for DevOps/test/org-intelligence vendors.
- Certification of host adapters or scenario packs against SFAEF conformance.

## Licensing constraint

Current source-available licensing may limit public marketplace distribution. Treat adapter licensing, specification licensing, and corpus licensing as separate owner decisions. Product packaging must be truthful and must not imply open-source status where the terms do not provide it.
