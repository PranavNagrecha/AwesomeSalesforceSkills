# SFAEF-210 — Commercial and ecosystem strategy

This document defines product boundaries, not final pricing or legal terms.

## Community value

SFAEF-210-001. A local user MUST be able to install, run a fixture, inspect evidence, and understand product limitations without a paid service.

SFAEF-210-002. Core result formats and benchmark methodology SHOULD remain portable and inspectable.

## Sustainable value

Potential paid layers include team governance, private evidence gateways, hosted benchmark orchestration, release-freshness service, enterprise policy/RBAC, private knowledge overlays, scenario packs, support, and implementation services.

SFAEF-210-010. Paid services MUST not weaken local safety or hide the basis of product claims.

SFAEF-210-011. Enterprise features SHOULD extend identity, collaboration, scale, support, and compliance rather than make local diagnoses intentionally unreliable.

## Ecosystem posture

SFAEF-210-020. Integrations with Salesforce, Gearset, Copado, Flosum, Provar, Elements, Salto, and other systems SHOULD use documented APIs/exports and preserve provenance.

SFAEF-210-021. SfSkills SHOULD publish a stable evidence/run export so other tools can consume findings without adopting the entire runtime.

SFAEF-210-022. A partner adapter MUST declare which product guarantees it enforces and pass conformance tests.

## Licensing decision gate

SFAEF-210-030. Public marketplace publication, open-standard branding, or claims of open-source compatibility require an explicit license ADR.
