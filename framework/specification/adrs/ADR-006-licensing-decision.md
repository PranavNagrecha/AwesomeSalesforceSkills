# ADR-006-licensing-decision: Separate licensing decisions by layer

**Status:** Proposed  
**Date:** 2026-08-19

## Context

SfSkills is evolving from a skill library into a Salesforce AI engineering framework and must make an explicit decision on this architectural boundary.

## Decision

Do not assume the corpus license automatically fits public marketplaces or an open specification ecosystem.

## Consequences

Owner/legal review required before public distribution claims.

## Revisit when

Two or more shipped products produce evidence that the decision creates measurable quality, safety, usability, or maintenance problems.
