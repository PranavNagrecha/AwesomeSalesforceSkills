# Licensing and distribution decision note

The uploaded SfSkills repository uses the PolyForm Small Business License 1.0.0. This specification package does not change that license or grant legal permission beyond the repository's existing terms.

Before public distribution, the owner must make an explicit licensing decision for each layer:

1. existing Salesforce skill corpus;
2. portable specification and schemas;
3. reference kernel;
4. host adapters and plugins;
5. hosted or enterprise services;
6. benchmark scenarios and captured evidence.

A practical option is to keep the corpus under its current source-available terms while considering a permissive license for a thin interoperability specification, schemas, or adapter SDK. That decision requires owner and legal review. Do not advertise public Cursor Marketplace availability until the current Marketplace license requirements and the chosen package license are compatible.
