# Well-Architected Mapping

Citation-first learning supports Salesforce Well-Architected outcomes indirectly:

| Pillar | Research contribution |
|---|---|
| Trusted | Separates guarantees, recommendations, assumptions, unknowns, security context, and org evidence |
| Easy | Produces terminology and concept order suited to the learner rather than a documentation dump |
| Adaptable | Preserves release, lifecycle, and applicability so learning material can be refreshed without rewriting unrelated concepts |

The six frontmatter dimensions are repository-internal mappings, not official pillar names.
